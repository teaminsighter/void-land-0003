How It Works

How My Top Agent Works

A simple, free and independent way to find the best agent to sell your property.

  1. Enter your details

    Tell us your property address, type and price range. It takes under a minute.

  2. Get your free property file

    We research recent local sales and activity around your address and send you a free property file.

  3. Receive your agent shortlist

    We email you the top 2-3 local agents we trust to sell your property. All this is absolutely free.

How we know who's good

Data tells you who sold the most. We find out who's actually good.

Sales data only tells half the story. We interview agents ourselves to understand how they communicate, negotiate, and follow through. Then we match you with those who consistently deliver, not just those with the best numbers.

So when we match you, it's an agent we'd trust with our own home.

Why Us

Why Use My Top Agent?

Six reasons Kiwi property owners trust us to find their top local agent.

Truly Independent

We are not a real estate company and do not have pre-selected agents. Every recommendation is based purely on our own research.

Backed by Real Data

We use independent sales statistics, not advertising spend, to find agents with a proven record of selling properties like yours, in your suburb.

Completely Free

The agent pays us a referral fee from their commission only if your property sells. It is always free for vendors, with no obligation.

Fast 48-Hour Turnaround

Tell us about your property, and we aim to send your top agent shortlist within 48 hours, so you can move quickly.

Your Data Stays Private

We have zero tolerance for spam and will never sell your information. Your details are only ever used to find your agent.

Local Knowledge

From a proven record in your own suburb to coverage across all 16 regions, we match you with the right local expert.
